# Screen Patterns

Use this reference for player-facing Next GM screens and screen reviews.

## Title And Save Flow

Purpose: sell the wrestling GM fantasy before administration.

Pattern:

- cinematic title-stage treatment
- franchise-save slots, not file records
- direct Continue, New Game, and Reset Save actions
- current career context visible when a save exists
- no marketing-page hero layout

## Setup And Contract Flow

Purpose: make the player feel hired, trusted, and under pressure.

Pattern:

- one clear emotional beat per step
- contract/rules/GM identity/brand/career preview/draft staged as a career start
- selected choice workspace with consequences framed as career identity, not hidden gameplay prediction
- clear back/continue controls
- no generic form-wizard feel

## Brand HQ / Home

Purpose: tell the player what needs attention next.

Required:

- brand scoreboard
- next show focus
- urgent decisions
- roster/champion/rivalry health
- IWC pulse or world reaction
- current show card or booking pressure
- clear primary action

Avoid:

- passive dashboard with charts only
- equal-weight cards
- no obvious next action

## Booking

Purpose: build a live television rundown.

Required:

- segment list/rundown
- selected segment composer
- runtime target/current runtime
- talent/rivalry/title context
- warnings
- projected effects
- save/simulate/advance actions

Avoid:

- plain form builder
- prediction spam before show
- no consequences
- no selected segment hero

Pre-show rule:

- show readiness and risk warnings only
- do not show predicted grades, fan reaction, finance fallout, title outcomes, rivalry movement, morale fallout, injury outcomes, social buzz, or Open Challenge opponents

## Draft

Purpose: televised war room under pressure.

Required:

- on-the-clock state
- selected prospect hero
- available talent board
- rival brand picks
- budget pressure
- roster needs
- scouting note
- make selection CTA

Avoid:

- static roster table only
- no rival pressure
- no timer/pick context
- no budget tension

Do not restrict open draft availability unless an active ticket asks for an optional restricted mode.

## Rivalries

Purpose: manage heat, story, stakes, payoff.

Required:

- active rivalry list
- selected rivalry spotlight
- heat/stakes/timeline
- projected effects
- story composer
- warnings
- payoff target

Avoid:

- generic relationship table
- no heat trajectory
- no payoff logic
- no storyline identity

Movement must be explained retrospectively after resolved events.

## Championships

Purpose: manage prestige and title ecosystem.

Required:

- belt/title cards
- champion
- prestige
- reign length
- defenses
- contenders
- title history
- title ecosystem health

Avoid:

- text-only title list
- no gold/prestige treatment
- no contender context

Only Match title matches can change championships.

## Locker Room / Roster

Purpose: manage talent health, morale, contracts, momentum.

Required:

- roster grid/table
- filters
- selected superstar tray/workspace
- morale/fatigue/injury/chemistry
- contract/cost context
- actions: rest, push, medical, manage

Avoid:

- plain spreadsheet
- no selected wrestler focus
- no human/team consequences

## Results / Show Recap

Purpose: payoff and fallout.

Required:

- show grade
- segment/match ratings
- fan/business impact
- title/rivalry fallout
- IWC/social reaction
- injuries/morale changes
- week transition

Avoid:

- dry summary
- no consequences
- no story fallout

## Week Review

Purpose: close the weekly loop before advancing.

Pattern:

- GM handoff with what changed this week
- roster fallout, title/rivalry updates, social buzz, finance impact, and next-week pressure grouped by meaning
- clear Advance Week action
- consequence language tied to resolved events, not invented offscreen story

## Wrestler Profile

Purpose: support GM decisions with character and history context.

Pattern:

- wrestler identity and current status at top
- TV history, pressure labels, championship/rivalry context, social mentions, and concise GM read
- current-state truth only
- no hidden truth, fabricated fallout, or raw-stat clutter without interpretation

## Calendar

Purpose: orient the player on the road to PLE.

Pattern:

- current week, go-home weeks, PLEs, completed shows, and season milestones
- no dumping ground for every alert
- clear next structural event

## Social / IWC

Purpose: living audience reaction after outcomes.

Pattern:

- feed items with source/category labels
- posts tied to actual resolved events
- contained scroll
- spicy but readable copy
- no pre-show buzz invented before the world has reacted

## Finance

Purpose: clear, gamey, decision-focused money readout.

Pattern:

- money, profit/loss, revenue, costs, pressure, trends
- gold for money/value, green for positive, red for deficit/danger, yellow for caution
- report what happened before projecting new systems
- avoid accounting-software layout

## Season Review

Purpose: season-ending broadcast package and executive review.

Pattern:

- actual gameplay history and season story summaries
- champions, rivalries, brand performance, finances, roster stories, and social highlights
- Start Next Season action visible
- no invented offscreen legacy
