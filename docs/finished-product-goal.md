# Finished Product Goal

## Purpose

This document defines the target finished version of Next GM.

It is a product target, not permission to add every system at once. Implementation still happens through bounded tickets that preserve the playable loop.

## How To Use This Document

Use this document to judge whether future tickets are moving Next GM toward the intended finished product.

Do not treat any pillar below as automatically approved implementation scope. Each feature still needs a bounded ticket, explicit scope, acceptance tests, and preservation of the current playable loop.

## Product Goal

Next GM should become a complete single-player, offline-first wrestling GM game where the player runs a brand across weekly TV, PLE cycles, roster pressure, championships, rivalries, audience reaction, finance pressure, and season legacy.

The finished game should feel like a premium wrestling management title: dark sports broadcast presentation, underground command-center pressure, franchise-mode clarity, and a living wrestling universe that reacts after the player acts.

## Core Player Fantasy

The player should feel like:

- A promotion executive protecting the future of the brand.
- A TV producer shaping the weekly show.
- A talent evaluator finding stars before the market does.
- A wrestling fan chasing hot moments, earned drama, and legacy.

The game should never feel like a SaaS dashboard, spreadsheet simulator, admin panel, chatbot wrapper, or generic CRUD app.

## Finished Gameplay Pillars

- Weekly TV booking that feels like building a live production rundown.
- Roster management with morale, fatigue, injuries, TV time, identity, value, and momentum.
- Championships that feel prestigious, historical, and strategically important.
- Rivalries that feel elastic, staged, and affected by real booking choices.
- Social/IWC reaction that responds to resolved outcomes and gives the world attitude.
- Finance that is readable, gamey, and decision-focused without becoming accounting software.
- Season and career continuity that makes each 50-week season feel like one chapter in a longer GM career, not the whole game.
- Rival brands that create pressure and world texture, only becoming simulated competitors when explicitly implemented.
- Scouting, contracts, free agency, and talent value systems that support the GM fantasy without overwhelming the core booking loop.
- Presentation that stages major moments — draft picks, title changes, PLEs, injuries, breakout stars, rivalry payoffs, and season endings — with more drama than routine management screens.

## Experience Target

Every major screen should answer:

- What is happening in the brand right now?
- What decision can the player make?
- What pressure or risk should the player understand?
- What changed because of past choices?
- What is hidden until after action resolves?

Consequences should come after action. Pre-show surfaces may warn and contextualize, but they must not reveal predicted grades, fan reaction, title outcomes, rivalry movement, morale fallout, injury outcomes, social reaction, finance fallout, or Open Challenge opponents.

## Non-Goals

This document does not authorize backend services, cloud sync, multiplayer, auth, payments, analytics tracking, external AI calls, broad rewrites, or unbounded system expansion.
